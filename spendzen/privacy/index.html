Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

SpendZen does not collect, sell, or upload your financial data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Monthly pool, fixed bills, expenses, notes, wish goals, and settings are stored on the device.
  • The Home Screen widget reads a snapshot from an App Group shared only between SpendZen and its widget extension.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. There is no server copy to restore.

Face ID

If you turn the lock on, iOS authenticates you on the device. SpendZen does not receive or store your biometric data.

Export

CSV or JSON export creates a file. It leaves the app only when you choose to share it.

Purchases

SpendZen Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. SpendZen does not receive your card number. Apple may tell the app that this Apple ID has unlocked Pro.

Privacy nutrition label

Data not collected. The app does not track you.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 24 日。SpendZen 不收集、不出售、不上传财务数据。没有账号，没有统计 SDK，没有广告。

月度总盘、固定开支、记账、备注、愿望和设置都在本机。小组件只通过 App Group 和 SpendZen 共享快照。删除 App 即删除这些数据。

面容 ID 由 iOS 在本机完成验证。导出文件只有在你主动分享时才会离开 App。专业版购买由 Apple 处理，SpendZen 看不到卡号。
