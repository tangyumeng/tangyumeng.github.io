SpendZen Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

SpendZen does not collect, sell, or upload your financial data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

Monthly pool, fixed bills, expenses, notes, wish goals, and settings are stored locally with Apple's on-device database and, for the home screen widget, in an App Group shared only between SpendZen and its widget extension. Deleting the app deletes this data. SpendZen does not offer an account that could restore it from a server.

If you turn on Face ID, authentication is performed by iOS on the device. SpendZen does not receive or store your biometric data.

Exporting CSV or JSON creates a file you choose to share. That file leaves the app only when you send it.

Purchases

SpendZen Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. SpendZen does not receive your payment card number. Apple may provide the app with the fact that this Apple ID has unlocked Pro.

Privacy nutrition label

Data not collected. The app does not track you.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page: SpendZen.

SpendZen 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 24 日

SpendZen 不收集、不出售、不上传你的财务数据。没有账号，没有统计 SDK，没有广告。

留在这台 iPhone 上的内容

月度总盘、固定开支、记账、备注、愿望和设置都存在本机。桌面小组件通过 App Group 只和 SpendZen 自己的小组件共享同一份快照。删除 App 就会删除这些数据。SpendZen 没有可以从服务器恢复账本的账号。

如果打开面容 ID，验证由 iOS 在本机完成。SpendZen 拿不到、也不会保存你的生物识别信息。

导出 CSV 或 JSON 会生成一份文件。只有你自己把它发出去时，这份文件才会离开 App。

购买

SpendZen 专业版是由 Apple 处理的一次性内购。SpendZen 不会收到你的支付卡号。Apple 只会告诉 App 这个 Apple ID 是否已解锁专业版。

隐私标签

不收集数据，不追踪。
