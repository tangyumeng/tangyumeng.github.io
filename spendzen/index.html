Example

133.3

A 7,500 pool minus 3,500 in fixed bills, across a 30-day cycle. The home screen shows 133.3, not 7,500.

How the number is made

Pool, minus bills, divided by the cycle.

今天的额度 =（月度总盘 − 固定开支）÷ 这个周期的天数。

1

Monthly pool

The money you can freely spend this billing cycle. Not your salary, and not the number on the home screen.

2

Fixed bills

Rent and subscriptions are removed before the daily number is calculated. Do not log them again, or the same bill is counted twice.

3

Today

Each expense lowers today’s number. Unspent money can go to Dream Stash, spread over the days left, or reset tomorrow.

Features

A small ledger around one decision.

记一笔、改、删、补记到某一天。不用再打开另一本账。

Log, edit, delete

Tap Log Spend, pick a category, and confirm. Open an entry to change it or delete it. A fresh entry can be undone from the banner.

Calendar

Tap a day and that day’s entries open under the month. Backfill onto that day instead of dumping it onto today.

Home Screen widget

The widget shows the same number as Today. −5, −15, and −30 queue a real expense and count toward the free entry limit.

Dream Stash

Money you didn’t spend can sit aside for something you actually want. One goal is free. Pro adds more.

Face ID

Optional lock for the daily number. iOS does the authentication. SpendZen never receives your biometric data.

SpendZen Pro

A one-time purchase. Free covers 100 entries, one wish, and the default theme. Pro removes the entry limit and adds export, more themes, and more wishes. No subscription.

On this iPhone

The ledger does not have an account.

Expenses, fixed bills, and wishes stay in the app. The widget reads a snapshot from an App Group shared only with SpendZen. Deleting the app deletes the ledger.

FAQ

Before you trust the number.

Why isn’t the home screen my monthly pool?

Home shows what’s left after fixed bills, split across the cycle. 7,500 with 3,500 in bills is a 4,000 pool, then about 133 a day in a 30-day cycle.

What if the month has no 31st?

A cycle start of the 29th, 30th, or 31st clamps to the last day of a shorter month, then returns to your chosen day when the month is long enough.

Can I delete an entry?

Open the entry and choose Delete this entry. You can also long-press the row. Today’s number updates immediately.

What happens if I delete the app?

The ledger is gone. There is no account to restore it. Export a backup first if you have Pro.
